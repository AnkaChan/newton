Added experimental cutting to `SolverVBD`: `ModelBuilder.add_cut_blade` registers a dynamic rigid blade with a capsule edge, and `ModelBuilder.add_cut_capacity` pre-allocates spare particles, spare surface rows and copy color groups. When the contact force on the blade edge along the cutting direction reaches the per-particle `vbd.cut_force`, the solver cuts the tetrahedral tissue along existing tet faces: a cut vertex keeps its particle on one side and gets a copy on the other, and the collision surface rows are rewritten in place. All sizes are fixed at `finalize`, so a frame with a cut stays one captured CUDA graph. New `cut_*` arguments of `SolverVBD`, `SolverVBD.cut_report()` and the example `vbd_cut_suture` (cut a block, pull the halves apart, sew the wound)
