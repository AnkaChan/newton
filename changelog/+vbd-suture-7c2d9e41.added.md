Added suturing to `SolverVBD`: `ModelBuilder.add_suture_needle` registers a rigid needle with its thread (an `add_rod` capsule chain) as one rod curve. When the needle tip presses a tetrahedral soft body above the per-particle puncture force (`vbd.suture_puncture_force`, `vbd.suture_puncture_cos`), the solver opens a virtual tunnel of barycentric tunnel points that follow the tip (device-side tet walk, exit through a boundary face). Each point holds the needle or thread with a transverse row (hard by default; `vbd.suture_stiffness` sets a finite wall stiffness) and the capped ALM friction of body-body contact along the tangent, with a per-particle grip force per unit length (`vbd.suture_grip`, `vbd.suture_mu`). Tissue particles near a tunnel are gated against the needle group in the soft-contact record filter and in rigid-soft DAT. New example `vbd_suture` sews two gripped elastic cuboids with a running suture, pulls the thread so the right block slides along its lower jaw until the wound edges meet, and withdraws that jaw so the block hangs from the stitches.
