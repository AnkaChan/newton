View the numbers

Frame = orientation

The red, green, and blue arrows on the grid are the cell’s orthonormal frame. Their origin is the actual deformed cell center.

Axes = deformation

The detail view keeps the three axis lengths and angles. In the cell’s own frame, they describe stretch and shear after removing the cell’s rotation.

Measured from the shape

Each axis comes from the difference between the two opposing face centers, divided by the rest cell size. For a warped cell this is the deformation gradient at its center.
