What happened

Newest first

    Different lengths, one optimizer

    Each instance samples a fixed K solver updates per physical step and H physical steps before reset. This short illustration uses K ∈ {1, 2, 4} and H ∈ {2, 3, 4}; the proposed training range reaches up to 32 and 128.

    A slot follows readiness

    When one instance finishes K, another ready instance can occupy that slot on the next batch. No instance is padded to 32 solver updates. If fewer than four are ready, the scheduler waits for preparation and records that wait.

    One physical state at a time

    After a full batch, one shared Adam update occurs and the proposals are detached. An unfinished instance returns ready. At K it prepares the next physical state; at H it resets and samples new K and H, without preparing an unused next state.
