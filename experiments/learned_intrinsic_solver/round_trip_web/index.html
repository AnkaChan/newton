What the reconstruction actually receives

Stored representation

Each cell stores its corner-average origin, its proper polar rotation R, and all nine entries of U. R and U are computed from the actual shared-corner shape. Multiplying them recovers the cell-center gradient F to floating-point precision; U retains stretch and shear.

The canonical grid supplies cell connectivity and rest spacing. The material z-min face supplies explicit global boundary positions. This report does not reconstruct from R alone.

Shared-corner solve

The decoder solves for displacement from the canonical rest grid with zero-start, undamped LSQR. Fixed corners are eliminated exactly. It receives no original free corners, original-shape initial guess, hidden anchors, or smoothing regularizer.

Gradient rows use the exact opposite-face-centroid formula. When origins are included, center residuals are divided by h and assigned the same dimensionless weight as gradient residuals. LSQR tolerances are 10⁻¹³.

Small residuals and shape recovery are different checks. The corner errors compare reconstructed corners against the original only after solving. The F and center residuals measure how well the reconstructed mesh reproduces the stored data. All metrics use actual coordinates, regardless of the display gain.

Secondary diagnostic: why exact general recovery is not guaranteed

A cell-center gradient does not retain every corner-warping mode. The final diagnostic adds δx(i,j,k) = 0.001 (−1)^(i+j) k/nz to the canonical shape. This changes corners by up to 1 mm while leaving every stored center, gradient, polar frame, and local-axis matrix unchanged to roundoff. It also leaves the fixed face unchanged.

The minimum-displacement decoder selects the canonical shape for that case. Even adding the frame origins cannot identify the missing mode. With h = 0.025 m, this constructed trilinear map has determinant at least 0.92 throughout each cell, so folding is not the cause.

The affine example is also informative: a minimum-displacement choice need not preserve an affine shape on an odd 11-by-11 corner cross section. Its invisible checkerboard component is removed. A different prior could choose a different representative; it would be an additional assumption.
