Each row compares three upright material cases side by side: tri_ke = 1e3, 1e5, 1e7, with tri_ka = 0.2 tri_ke. Rows use rho = c * rho_inertia, with no material-stiffness floor. Bending stiffness is 200 in every case.

The complete tall grid remains in one video, with every row synchronized. The selector scrolls to a row without hiding or replacing any row. The video is displayed at its native pixel size; scroll vertically to inspect lower settings and horizontally on narrow windows. The original movies remain available.

Settings: 360 frames at 60 fps, 10 substeps per frame, 10 VBD iterations per substep. The first second settles the bag; the next five seconds move its pinned rim. All cases share the same camera, initial scene, prescribed motion and 16-times-baseline self-contact storage, and evolve independently with retained multiplier history.

Metrics: stretch and bend describe physical deformation. Original force residual RMS is particle force balance after the final particle iteration of the final substep, before velocity finalization; it includes original elastic forces, damping, fresh native contacts and inertia, and excludes rigid-body residuals and DAT reactions. It is not a full-system KKT residual or an equal-state convergence benchmark. Median k_eff/k is shown for stretch, area and bending, in that order. Amber labels identify clipping or escaped contents; failed cases keep their last finite pose with an explicit red marker.
