ALM-DAT self-contact in SolverVBD: implementation review

Reviewed range: 4996f98f..986b7c92 on ankac/alm-dat-solver (6 commits, 2 new modules/tests, 2142 insertions), base = PR 4222 head e6ee0e2f plus the 35 elasticity-ALM commits. Stated intent: Anka's pseudo code and the ALM-DAT design notes (task folder AI-Logs/Newton/tasks/alm-dat/), narrowed by the decisions taken on 2026-10-02..04 (vertex-witness force row, canonical edge-edge record, planes placed from the committed pose). Reviewer: the author of the change, applying REVIEW_GUIDELINES.rst adversarially; every finding below carries its evidence, and probes that were run are named.

1. Big picture

Old behavior. With particle_enable_self_contact=True, SolverVBD detects vertex-triangle and edge-edge pairs once per step, applies a log-barrier penalty force (plus friction and damping) along the closest-point direction, and after every color of every iteration rebuilds a division plane per pair from the detection pose plus the accumulated displacement and clips each particle's displacement against it (_penetration_free_truncation, newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:3191). Contact forces have no memory; a loaded contact needs a stiff barrier to hold.

New behavior. particle_self_contact_method="alm_dat" (default stays "penalty_dat", that path is byte-for-byte unchanged) keeps one certified anchor pose per step and one stored division plane per detected pair. The VBD iterate moves freely (it may penetrate), a compliant ALM row per pair pushes along the closest-point direction with a multiplier that is carried across steps by matching the detector's sorted rows, and the accumulated motion is truncated against the stored planes after every N iterations and twice before the step commits. The committed state is the anchor, so every stored pair stays separated by at least the DAT separation band; a loaded contact rests on an ALM hard wall a thickness (hard_gap, default margin / 4) above the plane with lambda equal to the load, so a soft K no longer needs a barrier blow-up.

Verdict (details in section 9 and 11): Fits if narrowed. Opt-in, experimental, default untouched, 730 tests green; but one demonstrated defect (per-world reset wipes all worlds' contact history), one design decision that contradicts the agreed formula sheet (rho floored at K) and needs Anka's sign-off, an undocumented semantic (friction and damping ignored on this path), and a public surface of six parameters plus two methods of which only three carry the experimental marker.

One step through the pipeline (where each section lives)

2. Stored division planes: one certified plane per detector slot

Where this lives / what this part does. newton/_src/solvers/vbd/particle_dat_kernels.py:233-365, launched by build_contact_planes from _alm_dat_initialize and after every truncation. One thread per stored record of the detector's CSR rows (vertex rows then edge rows), strided like the existing per-pair kernels.

Old behavior. The old truncation kernel rebuilt a plane from scratch on every call (after every color), with no memory between calls, and processed both stored directions of every edge-edge pair.

New behavior. The plane is state: normal, point, effective margin and a validity flag per slot (ParticleContactDatPlanes, newton/_src/solvers/vbd/particle_dat_kernels.py:182-196). Normals come from the rigid-DAT certified separators (find_vertex_triangle_separator, find_edge_edge_separator), which test the closest-point axis, the face or cross axis and the edge-support axes and keep the largest certified gap. Only the canonical edge-edge record (e1 < e2) owns a plane; the mirrored record is marked invalid. Within one detection a pair that has become thinner than the separation band keeps its stored plane instead of losing it.

Why it is written this way. Slots are re-assigned by every detection, so the "keep the stored plane" branch is only safe within one detection; the solver therefore zeroes both validity arrays before the first build of a step (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:3399-3401). The "keep" branch exists because a pinched pair converges toward the separation band over repeated gamma rounds and the separator search then fails; dropping the plane there is how a strip crossed a plate during development (commit 09e9521f). The invariant that makes keeping safe: the committed motion never crosses a stored plane, so the anchor is still on the right side of it.

Subtle points. The stored normal is only a hint to the separator; when a different certified axis has a larger gap, the normal changes between rounds and the force row's gap changes definition with it (lambda is carried as a scalar). The refresh_normals=0 branch (slide only) is never selected by the solver (see R9).

3. Plane placement: the maximin offset along the normal

Where this lives / what this part does. dat_plane_offset (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/particle_dat_kernels.py:66-178), called from the plane builder with the committed positions and the remaining displacement trial - anchor. Pure function, no memory.

Old behavior. The paper's proportional split between the two closest-point witnesses, clamped so each side keeps 5 percent of the gap (place_dat_division_plane in the rigid DAT module).

New behavior. Every primitive vertex gets a "time to plane" line; the plane is placed where the slowest approaching vertex on each side gets the same fraction of its remaining motion. One witness per side reduces to the paper's rule. A side that does not approach gets no room. Each side keeps at least the separation band and at most half the gap.

Why it is written this way. The balanced fraction is a concave piecewise-linear function of the offset, so its maximum is at a crossing of one negative-side line with one positive-side line; with at most 3 and 2 vertices per side that is at most 3 or 4 candidates, each scored by the true minimum over all lines. Heights are measured from a primitive vertex (origin) for float32 precision at metre-scale coordinates, as the rigid-DAT separators do.

Subtle points. margin is passed as 0 by the solver (R7), so margin_eff is the separation band (about 1e-6 m at unit scale) except for pairs thinner than twice that. The maximin uses the full remaining displacement of a vertex that other planes may cap further; that is the coupling the repeated rounds in section 4 recover.

4. Truncation against stored planes and the committed anchor

Where this lives / what this part does. Three per-call launches in SolverVBD._alm_dat_truncate (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:3359-3389): truncate_against_contact_planes (per record, atomic minimum of per-vertex fractions), commit_truncated_displacement (per particle), then build_contact_planes at the new anchor. Called once at initialization, after every particle_dat_plane_update_interval iterations, and particle_dat_truncation_iterations times in finalize.

Old behavior. After every color, planes were rebuilt and the whole accumulated displacement from the detection pose was clipped; the committed positions were the clipped iterate, and the solve continued from the clipped positions.

New behavior. The anchor advances by a fraction sigma_i of the remaining displacement toward the trial, never past a stored plane (planar_truncation_t keeps dot(n, x - p) >= margin_eff), with the total displacement from the detection pose capped by the motion budget. The trial is left untouched; the solve keeps working on it.

Why it is written this way. Independent per-vertex fractions are jointly safe because half-spaces are convex and the motion is linear; the only coupling is through plane placement, which the next round re-does with the remaining displacement. The budget is measured from x_start, not from the anchor, because the detection's query reach is what certifies that no undetected pair can come into contact.

Subtle points. The budget clamp uses the triangle inequality, so a particle near its budget can be stopped short even when the exact clamp would allow more (R13). sigma is the solver's truncation_ts array, which the per-color materialization also fills with ones; the two uses never overlap in time.

5. The slack-gap ALM contact row

Where this lives / what this part does. alm_contact_row (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/particle_dat_kernels.py:550-579), the row geometry helpers (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/particle_dat_kernels.py:619-701) and the per-color scatter kernel (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/particle_dat_kernels.py:705-803), launched instead of the penalty kernel from newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:4041-4059.

Old behavior. Force magnitude from a log barrier active below half the margin and a quadratic blend above it, applied along the closest-point direction, plus Coulomb friction and gap-rate damping; the barrier's stiffness grows without bound toward the surface.

New behavior. One slack variable per pair carries a linear cushion of stiffness K = soft_contact_ke over the band hard_gap .. margin; past the hard gap the row is a wall whose stiffness is rho, independent of K. The multiplier is the force memory. Friction and damping are not part of the row (R3).

Why it is written this way. The force acts along the closest-point direction, as the penalty path does, and the stored plane normal only fixes its sign: a vertex that crossed the triangle reports a negative gap instead of a flipped normal. This replaced an earlier version that pushed along the plane normal itself; certified separators favour the largest-gap axis, which for laterally adjacent triangles is tilted or horizontal, and a frictionless resting patch drifted sideways (commit 5fb01828). The regime switch is continuous in force and at C = (lambda - K R) / rho, not at the plane, so a loaded contact rests in the band pushed by lambda.

Subtle points. rho is recomputed from the current barycentric weights at every evaluation and floored at K (contact_alm_rho, newton/_src/solvers/vbd/particle_dat_kernels.py:537-546); see R2 for why the floor exists and why it needs Anka's agreement. With a 1 kg vertex at 240 Hz the inertia metric is 57600 N/m, so there the row is penalty-like inside a sweep regardless of the floor, and only the wall term lambda - rho * gap distinguishes it from the old force.

6. Multiplier update and history matching

Where this lives / what this part does. _update_alm_contact_multipliers_kernel (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/particle_dat_kernels.py:807-866), launched once per iteration after the color sweep at the trial pose (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:4161-4182); _match_rows_kernel and _snapshot_rows_kernel (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/particle_dat_kernels.py:870-912), one thread per vertex or edge, at step start and step end.

Old behavior. No contact memory; every step and every iteration started from the geometric penalty.

New behavior. lambda <- transmitted force at the trial pose once per sweep, so the multiplier learns from the attempt even though the committed state never penetrates. At step end the sorted rows and multipliers are copied; at the next step start each element merges its previous row with its new row in one pass (both sorted by counterpart index) and carries matched multipliers, zero for new pairs.

Why it is written this way. Matching requires sorted rows, which is why the detector's sort_contact_rows is enabled on both construction paths (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:1138). The dual update runs before the plane refresh of the same iteration so the dual sees the same normal the sweep's forces used; Anka's pseudo code places it after the refresh, and either order keeps the attempt-fed property.

Subtle points. The non-canonical edge-edge slot also receives a matched value but no kernel ever reads or updates it. A capacity change re-creates the ALM state with zeroed history, so one step after growth starts cold (documented in code, not in the user docstring).

7. Solver integration: schedule, materialization, cadence, finalize

Where this lives / what this part does. Constructor validation (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:966-996), schedule default and validation (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:4756-4759, newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:4878-4882), the three private step helpers (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:3348-3412), the per-color branch (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:4155-4158) and finalize (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:4677-4684).

Old behavior. AUTO for the self-contact slot meant PRE_POST_INIT: detect at step start and again right after initialization, resetting the motion reference each time. Positions were materialized by the per-color truncation pass.

New behavior. alm_dat resolves AUTO to PRE_INIT and rejects any other schedule, because a post-init or in-iteration detection would reset the motion reference at a possibly penetrating trial pose. After each color the trial is materialized as x_start + displacement without truncation; the committed state is the anchor written in finalize.

Why it is written this way. The solve kernels only accumulate displacements; positions must be materialized for the next color to see them, which the old path did inside its truncation pass. Requiring at least one final truncation round is what makes the committed state certified; with zero rounds the output would be the truncated inertial target (that misconfiguration is rejected at construction).

Subtle points. Every call to _alm_dat_truncate passes refresh_normals=True, so the stored normal is re-searched at every refresh; the "slide only" cadence from the design is not wired (R9). _alm_dat_materialize_trial fills a ones-array per color only to feed apply_truncation_ts; a dedicated kernel would save one fill per color (R10).

8. Coverage flag, storage growth, reset

Where this lives / what this part does. record_contact_coverage (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/particle_dat_kernels.py:1068-1084) at step start, particle_dat_coverage_ok() (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:3414-3426), storage re-creation in particle_self_contact_info() (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:1237-1241) and the reset branch (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:2862-2868).

Old behavior. Overflow of the shared pair buffer dropped records silently for the penalty force too, but neighbouring penalty forces usually masked the gap.

New behavior. A dropped record means a pair without a plane and therefore without a certificate, so the detector's overflow flags are copied into a device integer every step (graph-safe) and exposed through a host accessor; tests show a pinched strip crossing a plate at the default budgets and recovering after check_and_grow_self_contact_buffers().

Why it is written this way. The flag is written on the device so captured graphs keep it current; only the accessor synchronizes. The reset shortcut was a deliberate simplification; its cross-world effect is R1.

9. Findings

R1Per-world reset clears every world's contact multipliers.

reset(state, world_mask) zeroes vt_lambda, ee_lambda and both previous-offset arrays unconditionally (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:2862-2868), while reset_particle_elasticity_alm on the line above honours the mask. Probe: two worlds of a patch resting on a plate, 60 steps, reset(world_mask=[False, True]): multiplier sum 7.57 before, 0.00 after; world 0 restarts cold for one step (its contact re-converges within the step, so the observable effect is a transient, not a penetration). The public reset docstring promises selected-world semantics. Priority P1 (behavioral, documented only in a code comment). Evidence: probe (e) of this review. A per-world clear needs the particle world of each row's element, which the element index already gives.

R2Rho floored at K contradicts the agreed contact policy and needs sign-off.

The formula sheet (alm_formulas.md section 6) fixes the contact-normal rho at the plain inertia scale with no stiffness floor; contact_alm_rho (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/particle_dat_kernels.py:537-546) floors it at soft_contact_ke by default. Evidence for the floor: a 6x6 patch resting on a level frictionless plate drifted 9 cm in 4 s at the plain scale (rho 72 N/m against K 1000) and 4 to 6 mm, same as the penalty path, at floor 1 or scale 10 and above; the mechanism is the stale multiplier transmitted through a 67 N/m Hessian pumping lateral oscillation in the coupled Gauss-Seidel sweep. The converged force is unchanged, but the fixed-point speed and the in-sweep character are not what the sheet specified, the 9K bilateral floor and adaptive growth were not compared, and the single-vertex resting test passes with and without the floor. Priority P1 (requirements: do not silently reinterpret; this is a design decision for Anka, not a defect).

R3Friction and damping are silently ignored on alm_dat.

soft_contact_mu and soft_contact_kd are model-level parameters that the penalty kernel honours and the ALM row does not; the parameter docstring (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:474-485) does not say so (only the changelog fragment does). A user switching methods on an existing scene loses friction without any warning. Priority P1 (undocumented semantic change; the behavior itself is the agreed phase scope).

R4Public surface is wider than its experimental marker.

Six new constructor parameters, but the .. experimental:: block names three; particle_dat_coverage_ok() and particle_contact_alm_multipliers() are new public methods on a public class with no experimental marking and no API-reference consideration; particle_self_contact_method is a string selector where the guidelines prefer an enum for a closed category (CODING_GUIDELINES.rst, "Prefer enums for closed categories"). Priority P1 for the marker (compatibility contract), P2 for the enum and for whether the two methods belong on the public class at all.

R5The "wall above the safety plane" claim holds only for one-sided approach.

The row's gap is measured from the closest point, not from the plane; the plane is placed by the maximin rule between the primitives. When only one side approaches, the plane sits at the other side's surface plus the band and the wall (hard gap from the surface) is above it. When both sides approach, the plane can sit farther from the lower primitive than the hard gap, so a contact pushed to the wall still reaches the plane and the scalar truncation scales its whole displacement, which is the damping mechanism the hard gap was added to remove (heavy incline: 13 percent of the analytic slide before, 67 percent after). Probe (a), two free layers settled on each other for 1 s, showed zero truncated vertices at rest, so the exposure is dynamic (mutual approach), not static. Priority P1 as a question: the docstring overstates, and a mutual-approach scene with truncation statistics is the missing evidence.

R6Canonical edge-edge records depend on both directions being stored.

The detector records each edge-edge pair from both edges' threads, and the plane builder keeps only e1 < e2. Under overflow the kept prefix may contain only the mirrored record, so that pair has no plane; coverage is already reported false in that case, and match_contact_history writes multipliers into mirrored slots that nothing reads. Priority P2 (consistent with the coverage contract; worth a comment and a test).

R7Truncation margin is hardcoded to zero.

self._dat_margin = 0.0 (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:995) means the design's thickness margin for the safety planes is never applied; the certificate is "no crossing plus the separation band", exactly what the old path guarantees. Judgment call, but the knob is dead and the comment is the only record. Priority P2.

R8Cost is measured only on toy scenes.

1.3 to 1.5x the penalty path per frame on 300 to 900-vertex scenes (plane build, sorted rows, per-color materialization, dual update, two final rounds). No demo-scale (cloth_twist, rollers) timing exists. Priority P2 (evidence request).

R9The slide-only plane refresh is dead code.

build_contact_planes(refresh_normals=False) exists and is tested at the kernel level, but every solver call passes True, so normals are re-searched at every refresh and the "SlidePlanes" cadence of the design is not exercised. Priority P2.

R10Per-color materialization pays a redundant fill.

_alm_dat_materialize_trial fills truncation_ts with ones before every color only to reuse apply_truncation_ts; a two-array add kernel would do. Priority P2.

R11Test coverage gaps.

No test for the solver-owned pipeline path (probe (b) passes), multi-world (probe (e) fails per R1), determinism, particle_dat_plane_update_interval=0 (probe (d): no penetration, 4.2 mm minimum height on the drop), reset() (probe (c) passes), or edge-edge planes under overflow. Priority P2.

R12Mutable detector shared with the pipeline.

With a solver-owned pipeline the solver sets sort_contact_rows = True on the pipeline's detector (newton/_src/solvers/vbd/solver_vbd.py:1138); harmless for other consumers (rows are a permutation) but it is a side effect on an object the solver does not own. Priority P2.

R13Conservative budget clamp.

The commit kernel scales by (budget - used) / |step|, a triangle-inequality bound; a particle whose anchor is already near the budget can be stopped short of a step that the exact clamp would allow. Only matters at the motion budget (half the detection slack per step). Priority P2.

11. Verdict by axis

Fit: Fits if narrowed. The outcome (non-penetrating self-contact with a compliant force that holds load without a stiffness blow-up) is a Newton solver concern and is implemented as an opt-in experimental mode on the existing solver with the default path untouched. Narrowing wanted before this becomes a public contract: settle R2 against the formula sheet; collapse rho_scale and rho_floor_ratio into one documented policy; decide whether the two diagnostic methods belong on SolverVBD or behind a private seam; mark every new parameter experimental.

Requirements: the agreed phases 1 to 3 (planes, switch, ALM row with matching) are implemented and evidenced; R1 is a demonstrated defect, R3 and R5 are documentation versus behavior gaps, R2 is an unagreed design change. Friction, damping, rho growth, the room-based normal choice and rigid-soft coexistence remain open by agreement.

Standards: R4 (experimental marker, enum, public methods); Towncrier fragment present and categorized; tests use unittest with imperative docstrings; no new dependencies; wp.array[...] annotations throughout; 730 tests green on cpu and two CUDA devices after the last commit.
