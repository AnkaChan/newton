## Representation (core)

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Per-cell targets | 21 = 7 vectors x 3: the three axes (columns of the centre deformation gradient F) plus four warping vectors (bilinear xi^b xi^c for the three pairs and trilinear xi^1 xi^2 xi^3). All seven rotate as world vectors under the frozen frame R_c, so the local representation is R_c^T [a_1 a_2 a_3 w_12 w_13 w_23 w_123]. `target_modes = 7`; `target_modes = 3` keeps today's affine-only behaviour for ablations and legacy tests. |
| Fusion | Per-Gauss-point target increments derived from the 21 values, so the per-cell fit is exact and only shared-corner agreement remains; unknowns (corners), system size, sparsity and factor caching unchanged; only the assembly changes. Energy path (F at 8 Gauss points from corners) unchanged. |
| State blocks | The five nine-value blocks become 21-value blocks in the same 7-vector basis (inertial offset, physical change, current gradient projected onto the 7 modes through the fusion adjoint, previous gradient, previous update). Node input = 21 + 5 x 21 + 14 flags + 2 scalars + 17 contact = 159. |
| Width | hidden 192, 6 heads (head dim 32), FFN x4, edge hidden 96; contact encoder unchanged (64). |
| Schema | feature_schema_version 6. |

## Data and training

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| Regime | Fixed-state epochs (2048 states), growth timetable as run 3, budget cap raised to 2048 (H = 128 -> K <= 16, H <= 64 -> K <= 32) so deep iterations stay in the mix. |
| Contact | kappa range [0.5, 10] (floor raised from 0.1). |
| Scale | Gravity magnitude sampled per state, log-uniform in [2, 40] m/s^2 along -y, so the dimensionless groups Lambda and Gamma are covered at fixed h and dt (equivalent to varying cell size and time step for the normalised network). Per-context gravity in the physics step. |
| Selection | Best checkpoint chosen on the full-horizon check (16 states, K = 8, H = 128, every epoch): mean final free-corner residual with survival required; final energy and deepest penetration recorded alongside. Cheap 64 x 8 validation kept for the curves. |
| Optimizer | AdamW 1e-6, clip 1.0, cosine 1e-4 -> 2.5e-5 over max_epochs 48, no early stopping. |
| Translation for unpinned bodies | Centroid Newton step with the rigid target recomputed from the current contact force (see notes/fusion-integrator-derivation.md); implemented AFTER the v4 launch as a follow-up because every v4 scene is pinned. |

## Deferred (after v4 results)

Depth [1,2,2,1]; flat neighbour lists; Newton collision pipeline and self-contact; GPU

### Trainer behaviour

- Network built with `target_modes` and `state_feature_dim(target_modes)`; `MixedHexSolverStep(..., target_modes)`.
- `_TrajectoryFactory.reset`: `|g|` from `SeedSequence([master_seed, seed, 4409])` (training and validation factories differ through `master_seed`; a fixed state keeps its gravity across epochs and resumes), `register_context(..., gravity=(0, -|g|, 0))`, `context_spec` mirrors `step.context_specs[key]` (gravity included, so checkpoints and the failure record rebuild the context), `metadata["gravity"]` (list) and `metadata["gravity_magnitude"]`, history blocks `empty_history(C, target_modes)`.
- Epoch diagnostics: `rank_0_material_ranges["gravity_magnitude"]` and a `material_histograms["gravity_magnitude"]` (log-spaced over the configured range).
- Selection: `_selecting_summary` picks the cheap or the full-horizon summary; `best_selection` records `source`, `metric`, `aggregation`, `completed_updates`, and for the full-horizon source `iterations`, `physical_steps`, `sample_count`, `physical_survivors`, `final_energy_joule` and `final_max_penetration_r` (each `{mean, median, max}`). Epochs without a full-horizon check are never selected under `full_horizon`. On resume, a changed budget of the *selecting* summary (`validation_full_count` / `validation_full_iterations` for `full_horizon`) resets the record.
- Under the growth timetable the full-horizon H grows with the stage (8 → 128 over the first ten epochs) and the mean final-step residual grows steeply with H (run 3, epoch 20: 30 N after 8 steps, 796 N after 128), so metrics at different budgets are not comparable. A full-horizon check at another (K, H) than the current record's therefore retires the record (`best_selection_history` entry with reason "full-horizon budget changed", the new K and H, and the retired record) and the comparison restarts at the new budget, exactly as a resume with a changed validation budget; under the v4 timetable `best_validation.pt` is rewritten at epoch 9 and tracks the (8, 128) check from then on. The record stores K and H.

### Validation / report / rollouts

- `mixed_validation`: the full-horizon summary gains `selection` = `{metric: mean final-step free-corner residual, eligible: no failure and every sample completed all steps, aggregation: FULL_HORIZON_SELECTION_AGGREGATION ("mean_final_step_free_force_residual_norm_n"), survival_required: True}`; the cheap record is unchanged.
- `mixed_report`: headline names the selecting source and its metric ("Selection metric (full-horizon check: ... after K = 8 learned iterations on each of H = 128 physical steps, epoch E): X N, eligible ...; Full-horizon survivors ...; Best so far ... (full-horizon check, K, H; final energy mean; deepest penetration mean / max)"), the cheap line is kept for comparison, the metric plot marks the selecting series "(selects)", and `epochs.csv` gains `full_horizon_selection_metric` / `full_horizon_selection_eligible` (appended after the regime columns).
- `simulate_mixed`: network and step rebuilt with the checkpoint's `target_modes`; each seed's gravity comes from its validation context (`metadata["gravity"]`, fallback: the step default) and is written to the rollout report together with `target_modes`.
- `mixed_training_reference`: network and step with the checkpoint's `target_modes`; contexts rebuilt from `context_specs` (gravity included).

## Implementation record — contact stiffness rule and load-based floor (2026-09-29)

Decision (Anka, 2026-09-29): contact penalty stiffness must be far stiffer relative to the
material ("if lambda/mu is 10^4 the contact stiffness should be around that as well"). With
`kappa in [0.5, 10]`, `E = 1e4` Pa and `h = 0.025` m gave `ke` of 125-2500 N/m per sample and
the softest heavy beams (`E = 1e3`, `rho = 1e4`, weight 6130 N) sank about a metre through the
floor at rest — the fall-through tail of the K8/H128 checks. Nothing committed; the running v4
campaign (`generated/training_v4_20260928`) untouched; the changes apply to the next launch or
resume. Details in `notes/contact-design-20260927.md`, amendments of sections 5 and 7.

| Item | Change |
|---|---|
| Stiffness rule | `contact_kappa_range` `[10, 1000]` in `generated/training_v4_config.json`: `ke` between `0.25 E` and `25 E`, centred on `ke = E` at `kappa = 1 / h = 40`. Dataclass and sampler defaults unchanged (older scenes stay reproducible). |
| Load-based floor | `contact_scene.contact_stiffness_floor(rest, density, gravity_magnitude, static_penetration_max, sample_radius)` = `m g / (n_face d_max)`, `m = rho C h^3`, `n_face` = largest exposed-face count of one material face (400 for 10x10x40), `d_max = static_penetration_max * r`. `sample_contact_partners` gains keyword arguments `density`, `gravity_magnitude`, `static_penetration_max`, `sample_radius` (defaults disable the floor; the draws are unchanged, `ke = max(kappa E h, floor)`, `kd = beta ke dt` from the floored `ke`). Canonical beam, `E = 1e3`, `rho = 1e4`, `g = 9.81`: floor 2452 N/m, binds for `kappa < 98`; `E = 1e5`, `rho = 1e3`: 245 N/m, never binds. |
| Records | `ContactPartners.ke_floor` / `floor_bound` (defaults 0 / False; `from_dict` accepts payloads without them). Scene metadata: `ke`, `kd`, `mu`, `kappa_effective` (replaces `kappa`), `beta`, `ke_floor`, `floor_bound`; `simulate_mixed` reads `kappa_effective` with a `kappa` fallback and reports `floor_bound`. |
| Energy floor | `MixedHexSolverStep.energy_floor` adds `c eps32 ke r^2 S` (`ke` of the context, zero for contact-free contexts, `S` exposed samples), the section-5 follow-up. |
| Config | `MixedTrainConfig.contact_static_penetration_max = 0.5` (`None` disables; must be finite and positive); `from_checkpoint_config` default `None` for configurations predating the field; v4 JSON sets `0.5`. `_TrajectoryFactory.reset` draws gravity before the scene and passes `material.density`, the gravity magnitude and `step.contact_radius`. |
| Resume | `_SCENE_SAMPLING_FIELDS` (`contact_kappa_range`, `contact_beta_range`, `contact_mu_range`, `contact_plane_probability`, `contact_plane_height_range`, `contact_max_points`, `contact_point_radius_range`, `contact_static_penetration_max`) may change on resume and are recorded in `report["configuration_changes"]`; the fixed-state regime re-samples every scene per epoch, so the change applies from the next epoch. With contact enabled the change also restarts `best_selection` and the controller's patience (reason `contact scene sampling changed`), since the validation scenes follow the same settings. `contact_max_pairs`, `contact_tokens_per_cell`, `contact_friction_epsilon` still reject. |
